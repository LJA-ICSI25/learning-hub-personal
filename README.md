# Going from Zero to Quantum Computing

## What is Quantum Computing and Why does it matter?
- A form of computing that uses Qubits instead of normal bits
- More effective on complex topics and simulations

- In classic computing, they use bits 
    - Bits are given 2 possible states
        - 0 
        - 1
    - Even supercomputers use this same process and logic as the first computers

- In Quantum Computing, they use Qubits
    - Due to how quatum particals work, they are able to consider every possible outcome from the start
    - They work with probabilites rather than binaries
        - More specifically, superposition
        - (At the quantum level, physical systems can exist in multiple states at the same time. Until the system is observed or measured, the system occupies all positions at once. This central principle of quantum mechanics allows quantum computers to work with the potential of the system, where all possible outcomes exist in a computation simulta- neously. In the case of quantum computing, the systems used can be photons, trapped ions, atoms, or quasi-particles.)
    - This process allow it to create simulations and problems far to complex for normal or even supercomputers
